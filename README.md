GLOBAL ARTICLES is a modern digital news website created to deliver accurate, timely, and well-researched information to readers around the world. The platform is designed as an independent online news source that prioritizes credibility, clarity, and accessibility. Its main goal is to inform the public about important global events while maintaining high standards of responsible journalism.

The website covers a wide range of news categories, including politics, technology, economy, health, sports, entertainment, religion, and documentaries. This structure allows readers to easily find news that matches their interests while ensuring balanced coverage of issues that shape societies locally and internationally. Each article is carefully categorized to improve navigation and user experience.

GLOBAL ARTICLES features a dynamic news system that highlights breaking news, latest updates, and top stories. Breaking news is displayed prominently to capture immediate attention, while the latest news section ensures readers stay updated with newly published articles. This system helps the platform remain relevant and responsive to fast-changing global events.

The project includes a secure admin panel that enables administrators to manage content efficiently. Through this panel, admins can log in, publish news articles, upload images, assign categories, and mark articles as breaking news.
